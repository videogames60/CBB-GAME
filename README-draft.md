# The draft

Two rounds, 60 picks, every offseason, before your roster decisions, so you
always know who's gone before you hand out raises. `dynasty/draft.js`; every
number is in `Draft.CFG`.

## Draft stock

**overall + 0.45 × upside** (potential above overall) **+ youth** (FR +4,
SO +2.5, JR +1, SR 0) **+ the scouts' noise** (±3, fixed for the year so the
mock draft is steady) and a little more on draft day (±1.5). A freshman
with the same ratings as a senior goes higher, as in the real draft.

## The field: league players and outside prospects

Not every pick comes from your league. About two-thirds of the real draft
is college players, so in the 352-team league about **40 of the 60** picks go
to league players, give or take a few a year; a smaller league gets
proportionally fewer (about 8 in the 74-team one). The rest go to prospects
from outside the league (international, G League, prep): placeholders with
a name and where they're from, whose stock is spread like the league
players', so now and then one goes top 5.

## Who's in

- **Seniors:** all of them. The undrafted just graduate.
- **Underclassmen** decide at season's end, on what they showed this season:
  - projected first round (pick 1–30): **declare**
  - projected 31–60: **test the waters**
- **Draft day** (after development): a tester still projected in the top 40
  stays in; the rest **withdraw and come back** (shown in the offseason). A
  player who declared and isn't picked is gone anyway (rare).
- A drafted underclassman's salary for next season comes back to your bank.

## After

- Every pick is kept in the **draft history**.
- **Draft reputation:** recent picks (first-rounders 1, second-rounders 0.5,
  the last 5 drafts, older ones counting less) add to the **development**
  part of your recruiting pitch: about 3 recent first-rounders give the full
  bonus (+0.35 on that factor's score). Coach development and facilities
  still matter most.
- **Prestige:** +0.5 per first-rounder, +0.25 per second-rounder, at most +1.5
  a year.

## Screens

- **Draft tab:** during the season, the live mock draft (your players
  highlighted, each underclassman's outlook: likely to declare / would test
  the waters; "League players only" for a small league), your players'
  projections, your program's draft record and what it adds to your pitch,
  most picks in recent drafts, and every past draft by year.
- **Offseason:** the roster decisions screen says who was drafted (round and
  pick) and who tested the waters and came back; the report shows your
  picks and the first round.

## Measured

Full league: 39–46 league players drafted a year (most top picks are
one-and-done freshmen), 7–10 testers withdraw, nobody who declared went
undrafted, and the mock draft called every first-round league pick. Compact:
8–11 league players a year (5–7 underclassmen).
